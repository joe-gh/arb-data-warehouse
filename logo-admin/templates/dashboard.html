Fake inventory

Third-party styles (any non-Arborwear mill) automatically sell with fake stock of 99,999 - the sync re-asserts it every pass. Entries here override that default per style: FAKE forces fake stock on a style the automatic rule treats as real, REAL forces true FDM4 inventory on a style it would fake. Changes reach stores on the next hourly sync.
